Harmonics Suite multiband gain-law adaptation

The fitted gain-law constants, depth curves and timing laws in
src/suite_ott_curve.h are adapted from the xfer_ott function by David Braun,
which explicitly declares an MIT license in Faust's compressors.lib:
https://github.com/grame-cncm/faustlibraries/blob/b8f0e061808aa320b899e82f81043164ace1d00f/compressors.lib

same library have different licenses and are not incorporated. Blocks supplies
