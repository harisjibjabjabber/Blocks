- FreePats synthesizer percussion, exact archive `SynthesizerPercussion-SFZ-20220718.7z`: archive `readme.txt` says Version 2022-07-18, names Roberto and Yoshimi/Geonkick/Audacity, and states **“Published under the terms of Creative Commons CC0 public domain dedication”**. Full archive LICENSE retained as `synth-drums-CC0-1.0.txt`; readme retained separately. These are recorded synthesizer outputs, NOT recordings of an acoustic drum kit.

CC0 text directly says owners wish to let the public “build upon, modify, incorporate in other works, reuse and redistribute ... in any form whatsoever and for any purposes, including without limitation commercial purposes”. Section 2 covers any current/future medium, any number of copies, commercial purposes; section 3 supplies a fallback licence. Consequently commercial Android bundling, raw publicly accessible browser files, repacking, trimming, format conversion and modified versions are permitted under the copyright/related rights controlled by the affirmer. This is redistribution permission, not merely royalty-free musical output. No required attribution, share-alike or source-disclosure condition. Keep provenance/credit voluntarily. CC0 section 4 excludes trademarks/patents and gives no rights warranty or assurance of third-party clearance. Do not ship included piano photo or provider logos: not needed and not separately assessed.

Full CC0 legal code also read with WebFetch at https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.en. A second raw HTML archive attempt returned HTTP 403; no local official-HTML copy is claimed. Full versioned archive licence texts above are retained instead.

The same file dedicates control files and example clips to CC0. That is NOT a sample licence. No permission request sent and no audio downloaded. Musical-output permission does not clear the planned bundle/browser hosting.

### VSCO 2 CE — legally promising, held for optional packs
Official https://versilian-studios.com/vsco-community/ explicitly applies CC0 to the community library and original WAV files and permits building instruments in any desired format. Official source reports 3 GB and 44.1 kHz 16-/24-bit WAV. Site heading currently 2.1.1; GitHub SFZ release is separately **1.1.0**, not asserted to be the same version. Sanctioned GitHub file read of `sgossner/VSCO-2-CE`, `LICENSE`, `ref=1.1.0` returned full CC0 1.0 Universal text, including commercial redistribution/modification language. https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE/blob/1.1.0/LICENSE . No archive downloaded. Do not treat third-party Kontakt/VST conversions or derivative libraries mixing other sources as automatically CC0. No instrument-specific layer/mapping claims verified.

### Salamander piano and drums — relicensing discrepancy, not a simple CC0 assertion
Author Alexander Holm's pages https://rytmenpinne.wordpress.com/sounds-and-such/salamander-grandpiano/ and https://rytmenpinne.wordpress.com/sounds-and-such/salamander-drumkit/ both explicitly say “As of 4.3.2022, this is now public domain!” Piano author additionally replied on 2 January 2026 that commercial edited-library use technically needs no credit because it is public domain. This is a public-domain declaration, NOT a verified CC0 instrument/waiver text. FreePats still identifies its piano derivative **V3+2020-06-02** as CC BY 3.0: 16 velocity layers, 48 kHz 24-bit, release noises/resonance, 707 MiB FLAC/1.18 GiB WAV (other 394 MiB WAV and 296 MiB SF2 options). Third-party mapping/processing rights may differ from original recordings. Hold, preserve both statements, and use explicit CC-BY-3.0 terms for an exact derivative if later selected rather than silently stripping attribution. No download.
